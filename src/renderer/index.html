  1. Download the CA Certificate by clicking the button above.
  2. Transfer it to your iPhone via AirDrop or email it to yourself.
  3. On your iPhone, go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and install the profile.
  4. Then navigate to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and enable full trust for the QuickClip CA.
  5. Scan the QR code above with your iPhone camera to connect.

You only need to do this once. After the certificate is trusted, your iPhone will connect automatically on the same network.
